Background

Ed.D. · media production

I'm a Senior Instructional Designer on ISC2's Continuing Education team, where I build AI-focused cybersecurity courses and the knowledge architecture and tooling that support them. Before ISC2, I managed a design team of four to seven instructional designers across four University of Missouri System campuses.

My path into ID runs through audio engineering and sound design for film — a background that shows up in how I think about pacing, layering, and attention. I hold an Ed.D. in Educational Leadership and Policy Analysis from Mizzou, and I write independently on media literacy and AI adoption for instructional designers.
