Instructional Design · Learning Experience Design Leader · AI-Powered Content Strategy

Designing judgment into the systems that teach.

AI is moving faster than most organizations can teach people to use it. I design and build the learning experiences, systems, and strategy that close that gap. I've spent 18 years learning every discipline it takes to do that well. 

 

My background spans instructional design, people leadership, multimedia production, content strategy, and strategic communication. As a Senior Instructional Designer at ISC2, I own the full design process for AI and cybersecurity learning content: SME collaboration, curriculum mapping, storyboarding, adult learning design, e-learning development, and project management. The challenge is translating dense, fast-moving technical subject matter into learning that holds up in practice. 

 

My work extends into research and writing, currently on how instructional designers build media literacy and evaluate and adopt AI. That work runs on the same engine as the design work. The frameworks I develop and the AI-assisted tools I build directly inform how I design, and the design work keeps the research grounded in application. 

 

The through line across all of it: setting the pace for how AI and learning design come together.

Background

Ed.D. · Instructional Design

Senior Instructional Designer and AI enablement leader with 18 years across instructional design, multimedia production, and team leadership. At ISC2, I design AI and cybersecurity courses and build the knowledge architecture and tooling behind them, including a custom AI agent that supports content audits, SME collaboration, and course development.

Before ISC2, I led a multi-campus instructional design team at the University of Missouri System, rebuilding the department's course development process end to end and cutting average build time from multiple semesters to one.

My path into ID runs through audio engineering and sound design for film, a background that still shapes how I think about pacing, layering, and attention in learning experiences. I hold an Ed.D. in Educational Leadership and Policy Analysis from the University of Missouri, and I write independently on media literacy and AI adoption for instructional designers.
